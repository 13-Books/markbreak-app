4 Getting started

Export & verify

The profile is the contract. The gate proves the break survived.

Export

MarkBreak is a profile of Pandoc Markdown. Send the manuscript through Pandoc to Docx (or your press path). Roles should surface as Pandoc Divs with role=…. Hard breaks must remain hard breaks — not soft suggestions.

Verify

On the open profile repo, the check fails when Word has no real page break: 

bash tools/verify.sh

Prefer fixtures over folklore. When a bug class repeats, extend the gates. See markbreak.com and Specification.

Where layout lives

The writer writes Docx and PDF from the manuscript. meta.md holds the publication record. layout points at the RigWorks page for trim and margins. Flow breaks stay hard breaks. Relay, Word, and InDesign still compose after that file exists.

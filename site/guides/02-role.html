2 Getting started

Set the role

A heading is not an epilog. Name the print matter in YAML.

One file, one role

Put a role in the front matter. Prefer this over mid-file markers. 

---
title: "Commit Don't Push"
role: chapter
---

# Genesis

Closed vocabulary

dedication · epigraph · preface · part · chapter · appendix · epilog · afterword · colophon · toc

Unknown roles are preserved — processors must not invent layout for them. Full rules live in the specification.

Many files

A book may be many chapter files with a spine list. Each entry should carry its own role. Numeric prefixes keep sort stable: 01-genesis.md, 99-epilog.md.
